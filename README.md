# PulseGuard

### "Spot it before it's too late."

PulseGuard is an AI-powered mobile health app designed to help users monitor their cardiovascular health and recognize potential warning signs before they become a serious medical emergency.

PulseGuard connects with existing wearable devices, such as Apple Watch, and combines multiple health factors into one easy-to-understand Heart Health Score.

> **Important:** PulseGuard is intended to support health awareness and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency care.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce preventable heart disease by helping individuals monitor their cardiovascular health, identify potential warning signs, and connect with professional care when appropriate.

---

## 👥 Target Users

PulseGuard is primarily designed for adults approximately **30–60 years old**, including:

- People who are concerned that their symptoms could indicate a more serious health issue
- People who don't regularly visit doctors but want to monitor their health
- People with a family history of heart disease or high blood pressure
- People who want a convenient way to keep track of their cardiovascular health
- People who want an organized summary of their health information to bring to a medical appointment

---

## ❤️ The Problem

Heart disease remains a major health challenge in the United States. According to the CDC, **919,032 people died from cardiovascular disease in 2023**.

Many health devices and apps already track individual measurements such as heart rate, activity, sleep, or blood pressure. However, these measurements are often separated across different features and platforms.

PulseGuard aims to bring multiple factors together in one place so users can better understand their overall cardiovascular health.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

PulseGuard combines information from wearable devices, user-entered information, and other available health data to create a personalized **Heart Health Score from 0–100**.

Potential factors include:

- ❤️ Heart rate
- 💓 Heart rate variability (HRV)
- 🫀 Resting heart rate
- 😴 Sleep quality
- 🏃 Activity level
- 🩺 Blood pressure, if available
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family history
- 🎂 Age
- 📝 User-reported symptoms

The app can analyze trends over time rather than relying on a single measurement.

If the system detects concerning patterns, PulseGuard can encourage the user to seek professional medical attention.

---

## 🚀 Planned Features

### 1. Heart Health Score
A personalized score from **0–100** based on multiple available health factors.

### 2. Wearable Integration
Use data from existing smartwatches and wearable devices to monitor available cardiovascular and wellness metrics.

### 3. Symptom Tracking
Users can manually enter symptoms that cannot be measured automatically by a wearable.

### 4. AI-Powered Analysis
AI will help organize and interpret collected health information and identify potentially concerning trends.

### 5. Daily & Monthly Reports
Users can view their cardiovascular health trends over time through easy-to-understand reports.

### 6. Doctor-Ready Health Summary
If the app identifies a situation that warrants professional attention, users can generate a downloadable PDF summarizing relevant information, such as:

- Symptoms
- Heart-rate trends
- ECG events, when available from a connected device
- Sleep
- Exercise/activity
- Blood pressure, when available

This could help users provide their doctor with organized information during an appointment.

### 7. Gamified Wellness Challenges
Users may be able to participate in challenges involving:

- Walking/activity streaks
- Sleep goals
- Healthy habits
- Blood pressure tracking

The goal is to make healthy habits more engaging and motivating.

---

## 🌟 What Makes PulseGuard Different?

Many existing health applications focus on individual measurements or fitness tracking.

PulseGuard's goal is to combine multiple health factors into one overall picture of cardiovascular health.

Instead of forcing users to interpret several disconnected measurements, PulseGuard aims to provide a simple way to understand trends and determine when professional medical attention may be appropriate using an AI assitant to ensure it goes well.
